Our Family

Three generations of the Mills family, working the room together.

Owner-Operators

Randy, Janis, Lesly, Mariela & Ryan Mills

Janis and Lesly Mills opened New York Hill Restaurant in 1989. Their son Randy, their grandson Ryan, and Ryan’s wife Mariela now work with them.

They take pride in working together in a way rooted in tradition, and they care about serving excellent food in a beautiful, historic setting.
